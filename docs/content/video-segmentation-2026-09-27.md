# Short video parts in existing lessons

The original curated catalog contains 13 lesson bindings to 12 unique
YouTube videos. A long source video should not force a learner to spend
13–22 minutes in one sitting. On 2026-09-27, the public YouTube watch-page
metadata gave the durations below. Muslingo now offers time-limited official
embeds, at most six minutes each, alongside the complete original video.
This is **37 viewing parts across the 13 bindings**, not 37 newly sourced
videos or 37 newly reviewed religious lessons. Idgham uses the same video
in two lesson bindings, so there are **34 unique time ranges**.

Each segment is a neutral numbered time range. We have not claimed that a
specific rule begins at an unverified timestamp, and we have not copied,
downloaded, or repackaged the media. The same source metadata, title and
publisher remain visible. The player uses YouTube's documented `start` and
`end` embed parameters; `end` is measured from the beginning of the original
video, not from the segment start. The original full video remains selectable.

This solves session length, not curriculum coverage. The Arabic and Tajwid
tracks still need more **distinct, topic-matched sources**; Quran and
Foundations currently have no curated video bindings. Before attaching
further videos to the 570 modules or 902 short Quran units, an editor must
record the exact source, duration, language, topic match, transcript or a
viewing note, rights basis and reviewer. Do not multiply a source across
unrelated lessons just to increase the displayed count.

Reference: [YouTube embedded player parameters](https://developers.google.com/youtube/player_parameters).
