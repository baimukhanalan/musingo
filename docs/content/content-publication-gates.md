# Muslingo content publication gates

The 570-module curriculum manifest is an editorial production queue, not a
claim that 570 religious lessons have already received expert approval.

Every module starts as `blocked_until_review`. A CMS may publish it only after
all of the following evidence is stored:

1. A named author and a precise learning objective.
2. Resolvable source locators and immutable canonical Quran text.
3. Rights covering the exact operation: link, embed, adapt, stream or offline.
4. The required Islamic, Arabic, tajwid, translation and audio reviews.
5. Language review for every published locale.
6. A content hash, source version, reviewer identity and review date.
7. Captions, transcript, speaker scope and provenance for every video.
8. A correction and withdrawal path when a source changes.

AI may help draft an exercise structure. It cannot mark a module approved,
alter canonical source material, invent a citation, or publish religious
guidance without the required human review.

YouTube and other third-party video stays in the official player or opens as
an external link unless Muslingo has written rights for native hosting and
adaptation. Audio, transcripts and clips are separate rights surfaces.

The generated manifest lives at
`docs/content/approved-570-curriculum-plan.json`. It is deliberately excluded
from the mobile asset bundle until modules pass publication review. Its target
distribution is Quran 150, Arabic 170, Tajwid 70 and Foundations 180.
