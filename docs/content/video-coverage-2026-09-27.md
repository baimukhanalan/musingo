# Video coverage and Academy lecture shelf — 2026-09-27

Muslingo keeps two different formats:

- **Courses** contain short interactive lessons. A video card appears only
  where a publisher lecture matches the lesson topic or a named example in it.
  The video title remains visible, so a single-letter example is not presented
  as a complete treatment of all letters in that lesson. Long videos play as
  provider-hosted time ranges of at most six minutes; the full original is
  separately available. Watching does not award XP or finish the lesson.
  Newly attached videos do not expose an auto-generated knowledge check from
  title metadata alone; assessments require expert-reviewed content notes.
- **Academy → Lectures** contains **220 complete, uncut external videos**:
  139 on reading the Quran, 51 on surah meaning, and 30 on prayer. These are
  separate from the 570 interactive Academy modules, not 220 newly authored
  Muslingo lectures and not full video coverage of all 570 topics.

The source IDs, titles, durations and individual publisher pages were checked
against the publisher's public [reading](https://abu-yasin.com/playlist/quran-reading/),
[meaning](https://abu-yasin.com/playlist/quran-understand/) and
[prayer](https://abu-yasin.com/playlist/namaz-understand/) playlists. Three
Kazakh foundation-topic videos were checked against their individual
[Muslim.kz](https://muslim.kz/videos) pages. They are embedded through the
provider's official player; no video files are copied or re-edited.

This is **source and topic metadata verification, not theological expert
approval of the newly added material**. The Academy explains that distinction
to learners. The original spoken language is displayed; translated interface
labels do not claim that the recording itself was translated.

Arabic's later letter units have a source-specific video at the first lesson
of each three-lesson cycle. Foundations and Tajwid are near or above the
requested approximate one-in-three/four density. Quran is **not** there yet:
most of its 902 complete-Quran units are short, canonical reading/listening
practice rather than expository video lessons. Surah-specific videos cover
only the verified matches. Filling every fourth Quran unit would require
hundreds of additional precisely matched, reviewed sources; recycling a
lecture across unrelated ayahs would mislead learners.

Next editorial work: source and review additional surah-specific lessons and
full lectures for Academy modules not yet represented; document language,
exact topic/verse range, speaker, duration and independent expert decision
before making theological claims or attaching an assessment to new media.
